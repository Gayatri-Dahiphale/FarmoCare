Cotton

(Gossypium hirsutum)

Indeterminate annual sub-shrub cultivated as commercial fiber. Highly sensitive to atmospheric vapor pressure deficits and subsurface salinity during the squaring and boll enlargement period.

timeline Phenological Timeline (BBCH) 160–180 Days Cycle
Phase 0-1: Emergence & Seedling 0 - 15 DAP

Radicle emergence, cotyledon unfolding. Base temp threshold: 15.5°C.

Phase 2: Monopodial & Sympodial Vegetative 16 - 45 DAP

Rapid stem elongation. Main stem nodes 5 through 9 establish fruiting branches.

Phase 5-6: Squaring & Peak Flowering CURRENT REGION

Formation of pinhead squares (flower buds). Critical pollen viability susceptible to temp >36°C.

Phase 7: Boll Development & Fiber Filling 80 - 130 DAP

Cellulose deposition inside boll locules. Highest potassium requirement window.

Phase 8-9: Boll Dehiscence & Harvest 135 - 170 DAP

Carpel splitting, lint drying. Zero precipitation needed for clean lint picking.
