Transparency

Frequently Examined Questions

How does Farmora prevent commercial chemical bias in recommendations?

expand_more

Farmora operates strictly on an independent scientific charter. We do not accept brand-sponsored commissions from pesticide manufacturers. All recommendations are issued by active generic molecule names with strict minimum biological threshold criteria.

What is the turnaround time for acute pest infestations?

expand_more

Emergent flags (such as Spodoptera or severe blight) trigger immediate priority routing. Our human-in-the-loop regional specialist review operates with a contractual SLA of under 120 minutes during daytime field operational hours.

Can offline field scouts sync data in remote areas with zero cell reception?

expand_more

Yes. The Farmora Scout application caches all photographic evidence, geolocation tags, and symptom matrices locally on the device, queueing the batch for automatic dispatch upon detecting cellular or satellite network reconnection.
