## Brand & Style

This design system embodies premium organic minimalism tailored for high-precision agricultural advisory and stewardship. It balances tactile earthy authenticity with the disciplined clarity of modern enterprise software. The visual language conveys quiet authority, scientific rigor, and deep environmental attunement.

Designed for modern agronomists, farm enterprise operators, and soil consultants, the interface rejects cold techno-futurism and chaotic dashboard clutter. Instead, it employs generous breathing room, high-contrast editorial typography, muted organic surfaces, and purposeful botanical accents. Interactions feel grounded, stable, and deliberate—inspiring trust and long-term stewardship.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural life cycle of fertile soil and botanical growth:
- **Primary (`#658665` - Sage Green)**: Dedicated to focal actions, key transactional triggers, and validated active states. It suggests vitality without harsh artificial saturation.
- **Secondary (`#5C4F2D` - Earth Brown)**: Grounds the hierarchy, anchoring secondary workflows, qualitative metric labels, and key soil/land management accents.
- **Tertiary (`#B6B243` - Olive Accent)**: Applied sparingly to draw attention to seasonal transitions, advisory notices, and specialized alerts.
- **Neutral (`#0C0D05` - Deep Ink)**: Delivers uncompromising contrast against light backgrounds for primary typography, fine strokes, and structural wirelines.
- **Canvas & Surfaces (`#FBFBFB`, `#F3F2EB`)**: Provide an organic off-white canvas with warmth, preventing screen glare during field or outdoor operation.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5E4DC`)**: Define boundaries with whisper-quiet precision, maintaining layout structure without visual weight.

## Typography

Manrope serves as the unified typographic engine across editorial titles, functional body copy, and precision data tables. 

- Large titles feature geometric discipline with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver an editorial presence reminiscent of high-end agricultural journals.
- Body copy balances open aperture design with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to maintain legibility under varying ambient lighting conditions.
- Functional labels and telemetry values use semi-bold weights with slight positive tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to establish visual separation and instantaneous scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px modular cadence arranged within a responsive 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop):
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with 24px gutters and 48px outer canvas margins. Large analytical blocks span 4 or 6 columns; wide sensor telemetry spans 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Sidebars collapse to top navigation or modular slide-outs.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px canvas margins. Analytical cards stack vertically with persistent single-tier action bars.

Component internal gaps strictly follow standard multiples of 8px (8px, 16px, 24px, 40px), preserving an uncluttered rhythm between analytical widgets and narrative advisory cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface layering and architectural low-contrast borders rather than pronounced drop shadows.

- **Surface Tiers**: Base canvas rests at `#FBFBFB`. Elevated content containers, cards, and toolbars sit on `#FFFFFF` or `#F3F2EB` depending on contextual contrast needs.
- **Structural Framing**: Crisp `1px` solid borders in `#E5E4DC` delineate widgets and section boundaries, maintaining order without visual noise.
- **Ambient Shadow Depth**: Floating dropdowns, contextual popovers, and sticky overlays employ a soft, warm ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(12, 13, 5, 0.04)`. Hover states on interactive cards gently transition the border tone to `#658665` rather than introducing harsh elevations.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances modern precision with the soft fluidity of organic forms. 
- Primary containers, analytical panels, and operational cards adopt 16px (`rounded-lg`) to 20px (`rounded-xl`) corner radii, generating a refined, approachable envelope.
- Badges, status markers, chips, and primary buttons employ a continuous full-pill geometry (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular dashboard modules.
- Form inputs and text fields standardize on 10px to 12px radii for structural stability and clean alignment with data tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Sage Green (`#658665`) fill, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with 12px vertical and 24px horizontal. Hover states shift background to `#547354` with a smooth 150ms ease transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1.5px border in `#5C4F2D` or `#E5E4DC`, text in `#0C0D05`. On hover, background shifts to `#F3F2EB`.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- Pill-shaped badges (`rounded-full`) with `label-sm` typography. 
- Positive advisory tags utilize 12% opacity `#658665` backgrounds with dark sage text.
- Warning or nutrient depletion alerts utilize `#B6B243` at 15% opacity with `#5C4F2D` text, ensuring WCAG AA contrast against `#FBFBFB`.

### Cards & Analytical Widgets
- Base background `#FFFFFF` or `#F3F2EB` with a continuous `1px` border in `#E5E4DC` and 18px radius.
- Internal padding is set to 24px (`space-lg`). Headers within cards feature clean divider rules (`#E5E4DC`) separating metric telemetry from narrative recommendations.

### Form Inputs
- Single-line inputs and selects feature a `#FFFFFF` canvas, `1px` border in `#E5E4DC`, 12px radius, and 12px 16px internal padding.
- Focus state replaces the border with `#658665` and a subtle 2px outer ring tinted at 15% opacity (`rgba(101, 134, 101, 0.15)`).

### Data Tables
- Header cells styled in `#0C0D05` at 70% opacity using `label-sm` uppercase styling, sitting atop `#F3F2EB` sub-headers.
- Data rows feature 16px vertical padding with fine bottom borders in `#E5E4DC`. Alternate striped rows are avoided in favor of clean white space and subtle row-hover highlights in `rgba(243, 242, 235, 0.6)`.
