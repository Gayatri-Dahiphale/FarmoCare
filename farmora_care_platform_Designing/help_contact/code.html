Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to critical inquiries regarding response SLAs, offline field usage, regulatory compliance, and agronomic credentials.

Cases submitted via the Toll-Free Helpline (1800-XXX-XXXX) during operating hours (6:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST) are triaged live with an agronomist within 3 to 5 minutes. For inquiries submitted through the digital inquiry form or WhatsApp incident queue, automated computer vision prescreens symptoms in under 90 seconds, and a district-assigned extension agronomist verifies and signs off on your tailored docket within 45 minutes. Severe blight or epidemic alerts receive top queue priority.

Farmora Care is deliberately engineered for rural connectivity constraints. Farmers can simply place a direct voice call to our toll-free line without requiring mobile data. Additionally, our mobile portal supports an Offline First Mode that stores your logged readings, leaf photographs, and soil observations locally on your device; as soon as your device reconnects to 2G or intermittent EDGE signals, the data bundles are compressed and dispatched asynchronously to your local extension station.

Every bio-stimulant, fertilizer dilution ratio, fungicide, and pesticide recommendation formulated by Farmora Care strictly follows the statutory guidelines established by the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC) and the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare. We enforce strict Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) warnings and Maximum Residue Limits (MRL) to protect farm safety, consumer health, and export eligibility.

Agronomists, soil scientists, plant pathologists, and certified extension officers with a recognized B.Sc./M.Sc. in Agriculture or ICAR credentials can apply through our Expert Onboarding Pathway. Candidates submit their academic verification, regional cluster preference, and licensing documentation. After a peer review by our Senior Scientific Advisory Council, selected experts receive access to our telemetry triage console, diagnostic AI tools, and localized grower advisory panels.
