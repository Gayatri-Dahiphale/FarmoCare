Best Practices for Quality Reports

Advisory accuracy depends directly on photographic clarity and environmental metadata. Follow these four standards when creating reports.

photo_camera

15–20 cm Distance

Hold camera steady 15 to 20 cm from symptomatic margins. Keep both affected and healthy transitions in focus.

Natural Diffuse Light

What is the certified advisory turnaround window?

expand_more

Preliminary automated triage happens within 90 seconds. Full agronomist-certified prescription protocols are returned in under 2 hours during standard operating hours (06:00 to 20:00 IST), and under 4 hours overnight.

How are contagious emergency outbreaks prioritized?

expand_more

If triage flags high-risk vectors (e.g., Fall Armyworm, Desert Locust, or late potato blight with wet weather projections), your ticket triggers Tier-1 Emergency routing, pinging nearby extension officers and local mandi bulletin centers.

Is Farmora available in regional languages and voice formats?

expand_more

Yes. The submission workflow and final advisories support Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Gujarati, and Bengali. Farmers can listen to voice-narrated treatment plans directly via WhatsApp audio notes.
