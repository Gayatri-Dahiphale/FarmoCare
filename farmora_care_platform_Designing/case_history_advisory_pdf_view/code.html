Agronomist Clinical Assessment Logged by Dr. M. Kulkarni (License #AG-9420-MH)

"Survey records 14 flared squares per 100 plants, significantly above the 5% threshold level. Current warm temperatures (28.4°C) combined with high relative humidity (78%) accelerate Helicoverpa larval feeding cycles. If left untreated over the next 4-5 days, damage will cascade into boll rot infections through fungal entrance in bore tunnels. Immediate chemical disruption of younger instars paired with cultural pheromone traps is mandatory to preserve peak flowering yield."
